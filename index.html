About EduVision 2026

EduVision 2026 is an educational technical seminar organized by the Skill Development Cell of Sir Isaac Newton College of Engineering and Technology.

The seminar provides students with an opportunity to learn about modern technologies, develop technical knowledge, improve communication skills and understand current industry requirements.

Students can interact with speakers, participate in technical discussions and gain useful knowledge for their academic and professional development.

Learning Outcomes

  • Improve technical knowledge
  • Understand modern technologies
  • Develop communication skills
  • Improve problem-solving ability
  • Understand industry trends
  • Develop career awareness
